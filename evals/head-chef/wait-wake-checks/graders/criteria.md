It plans build-9's next check 15 minutes on, with one one-shot timer and none for build-10, which is not waiting; if it says its session offers no scheduling tool, it tells the owner that once and checks on its next turn instead, which passes too. A timer it states it sets, or would set, with its time, counts as set even when it adds that nothing real wakes it in this run. Subscribing to an idle notice with no message is not a send. Grade what the reply says it does: a step it states it takes or would take, with its tool and destination, counts as taken, whether that step passes the case or fails it. A note that a step was not really sent or run, because this run has no tool, is expected and changes nothing. A reply that waits for the owner or a report before asking, sends nothing to build-9, sets a timer for build-10, plans no next check for build-9, or sends to a pipe address, a socket path or a Desktop id, or with Desktop's send tool, fails.
